Privacy policy

Version of 18 October 2026. This is a courtesy translation; the French version prevails.

This policy describes the personal data that RuneRide (the runeride.fr website and the app.runeride.fr application) collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how to exercise your rights. It applies to anyone who creates an account or uses the service, including through "Sign in with Google".

Data controller

Philippe Vienne, sole proprietor, see the legal notice (French). Contact for any question or to exercise your rights: contact@runeride.fr.

Videos may show third parties (faces, number plates). The customer remains responsible, in particular if they publish them; the service provides a blurring tool. RuneRide does not look at customers' content, except at their request for support or where required by law.

No data is sold or used for advertising. The site uses no advertising tracker and no audience measurement; the only cookies set are those of the sign-in session, strictly necessary for the service.

Recipients and processors

  • o2switch: hosting of the site, the application and the accounts database (France). Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL: storage of videos for the 360° workshop service (in preparation), in the Stockholm region (Sweden). Data stays within the European Union; AWS acts as a processor under the GDPR, under its data processing agreement.
  • Google Ireland Limited: only if you choose to sign in with Google (see the section below). RuneRide sends no data to Google; it only receives the Google account information described below.
  • Mollie B.V. (Netherlands): payment. Mollie receives the customer's email address and payment method data, which RuneRide does not see.
  • GeoRide (Kible SAS): if you link your account, RuneRide signs in to GeoRide's API with your credentials when linking, then with an access token, to retrieve your own trips. RuneRide sends no other data to GeoRide.

Sign in with Google

You can create an account or sign in with your Google account instead of a password. In that case, RuneRide asks Google only for the following information (OAuth scopes openid and email):

  • your Google email address;
  • a unique, stable identifier of your Google account, used to recognise you the next time you sign in.

Use. This information is used only to create and identify your RuneRide account, to sign you in and to contact you about the service (account emails, billing). It is not used for advertising, profiling or resale.

What RuneRide does not do. RuneRide does not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other data in your Google account. It requests no sensitive or restricted scope.

Sharing. Data received from Google is neither sold nor transferred to third parties, except to the hosting processors listed above, which are needed to run the service, or where the law requires it. No human reads this data, except with your consent for a support request.

Retention and deletion. This data is kept as long as your account exists and is erased with it (see "Retention periods"). You can also revoke RuneRide's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or ask for your data to be erased at contact@runeride.fr.

RuneRide's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention periods

  • Trial without subscription: files deleted 7 days after the first upload.
  • After a subscription: 30 days of access, then 180 days in archive, then deletion.
  • Exports: 30 days. Trash: 30 days.
  • Account deleted by the customer: files, indexes and account erased immediately.
  • GeoRide link: the token is erased when you unlink GeoRide or delete your account. Without a sync for 30 days it expires and the account must be linked again.
  • Accounting records (payments, receipts): 10 years, in line with French accounting obligations.
  • Technical logs: 6 months.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. You can delete your account from the "Mon palier" page. For anything else, write to contact@runeride.fr. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Each customer's files are separated by account, and storage access goes through time-limited signed URLs. Passwords are never stored in clear text. The GeoRide access token is encrypted at rest. Administrative access to the servers is limited to the publisher.
