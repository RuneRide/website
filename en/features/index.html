Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. RuneRide runs in a recent browser, on computer or phone, and can be added to the home screen.

Which cameras does the 360° editor support?

The Insta360 X5 (.insv and .lrv files). Other models will follow depending on demand.

Is my data sold?

No. Your rides, positions and videos are only used to provide the service. No advertising tracker and no analytics on this site. See the privacy policy (French only): confidentialité.

Can I use it without the online service?

Yes, the software is free: the code is on GitHub.
