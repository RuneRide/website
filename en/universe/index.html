The Pact of the Twin Runes

In the Old Age, two realms rarely spoke. The Dwarves of the Anvil, masters of furnaces, iron and stone, cut the passes into granite to haul ore. The Elves of the Canopy, masters of wind, cartography and optical glass, wove trails that follow the earth without breaking a branch.

When the Great Blight of Rust and Isolation threatened to sever the realms, both kindreds sealed a truce at the High Pass of Raidho (ᚱ). The Dwarves forged the Iron Steeds, hearts of controlled fire and bones of tempered steel. The Elves bound them with Sylvan Sight: crystal lenses that capture every angle of the world, and wayfinding runes that whisper the path through the mist.
