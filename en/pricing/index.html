Good to know

Free trial of the workshop

7 days from the first upload, with 128 GB and 10 minutes of export. Without a subscription at the end, uploaded footage is deleted.

Credits

Sold in packs of 60 or more (€4.20), they do not expire. They pay for export minutes beyond the monthly ones and for storage above quota.

After a subscription

Your footage stays accessible 30 days, then is kept 6 months in archive. Retrieving it costs €3 per 100 GB, by taking a plan again.

Contractual details are in the terms of sale (French only; draft under review).
